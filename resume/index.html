About

I completed my MSc (2023–2025) at the University of Calgary in the Predictive & Intelligent Systems Lab under Prof. Mustafa Mohamad. My thesis, Lagrangian Data Assimilation Algorithms for High-Dimensional Systems, developed methods for state and parameter estimation in high-dimensional nonlinear and turbulent dynamical systems using Lagrangian tracers.

I have a BSc in Computer Engineering from An-Najah National University (2018–2023). Professionally I have worked as a Python Engineer (ITG Software), Machine Learning Engineer Intern (Harri), and on research projects spanning scientific computing, time-series, and computer vision.

I am currently focused on real-time perception systems — LiDAR processing, multi-sensor fusion, edge computer vision, and the production Linux systems that keep autonomous platforms reliable. I am open to roles in computer vision, robotics software, and scientific ML across Canada.
