Problem

Most particle demos hide the force field. Wake makes the field the product: pointer position becomes attraction, a click becomes a shockwave, and trails show the integration.

What it is / is not

It is an interactive canvas field. Move the pointer to swirl motes; click to burst them outward. It is not a physics engine paper and not a GPU particle library.

Architecture

pointer / click
    ↓
force field
    ↓
particle integration
    ↓
trails + color shift
    ↓
Canvas 2D

Implementation

TanStack Start, React, Tailwind, Canvas 2D. All simulation stays on-device.
